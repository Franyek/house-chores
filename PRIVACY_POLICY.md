## Data Collection

## Data Storage
All chore information is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard iOS data storage. This data:
- Remains on your device only
- Is never transmitted to any server
- Is never shared with third parties
- Is backed up via iCloud if you have iCloud Backup enabled (controlled by your iOS settings)
